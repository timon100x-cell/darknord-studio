# Shot Grammar

Last checked: 2026-11-12 (source: docs/research.md)

## Timing on the bar grid

- In 4/4, one bar lasts **240 ÷ BPM** seconds. At 90 BPM: 2.67 s.
- Give each shot **one, two or four bars**.
- Hold wides longer than close-ups. Tighten toward each climax.
- A 3:30 song at 90 BPM has about 79 bars; two bars per shot gives roughly 40 shots.
- Country cuts on bars, slower than pop. Cut at beat level only at climaxes.

## Cutting

- Cut on downbeats. At climaxes, cut a few frames early for surprise.
- Speed up cutting toward climaxes in verses and choruses; slow it in bridges.
- Change place or light exactly at section boundaries.
- Bridge: longest takes. Final chorus: fastest cutting, with callbacks.
- Outro: one held shot.

## Framing (western grammar)

| Shot | Use |
|---|---|
| Extreme wide, small figure | Personal drama becomes fate |
| Extreme close-up (eyes, skull, hands, one object) | Cut straight against the wide |
| Frame in a frame (doorway, window) | A character who cannot belong |
| Silhouette against fire or low sun | Iconic, thumbnail-ready |
| Slow push-in on a still figure | Pressure, decision |
| Locked camera | Default; reads as more cinematic than a needless move |

Every shot in the list states: bars, shot size, action, purpose, and its lyric link
(illustration, amplification or disjuncture).

## Singing shots

Grok cannot sync lips to your own track. Default: keep mouths unreadable (profiles,
wides, backs, hat shadow, hands on the instrument). See `prompts/grok-video.md`.

## Risky shots (moderation)

Clips are reportedly moderated more strictly than stills. Show the moment before and
the aftermath in plain physical words, and keep an alternate shot for every risky one.

## Production order

1. **Bible.** Character, wardrobe, palette, one lens-and-grain line. Reuse word for word.
2. **References.** Three to five stills per recurring character and main location.
3. **Timing map.** Section timestamps and BPM.
4. **Shot list** on the bar grid.
5. **Stills.** Judge each: right subject, every named element, right count, bright enough.
6. **Motion.** Animate only approved stills. Mute the generated audio.
7. **Edit.** Cut to the song.
8. **Silent check.** Watch once with the sound off. If the story does not read, fix the
   shot list, not the prompts.
