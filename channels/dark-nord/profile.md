# Dark Nord — Channel Profile

Last checked: 2026-11-12

Dark Nord fuses dark country, Nordic/Viking folk and doom atmosphere. Songs are made in
Suno. This file holds what is specific to the channel. General Grok prompting rules
live only in `.claude/skills/prompts/grok-image.md` and `grok-video.md`.

## The one recurring character: the Skeleton Cowboy

He is the only thing that returns from video to video. He takes a new situation in
every song but keeps the same silhouette and marks.

**Character block.** Paste this word for word as the second sentence of every still
prompt he appears in. Never paraphrase, shorten or add to it.

> He has a cracked skull, a broken-brimmed cowboy hat over sparse long hair, a dark bandana around his neck, a tattered brown leather duster and black gloves.

- **Energy:** powerful, menacing, defiant, never passive. He commands the frame.
- **Readable skull:** the light must always keep the skull and its cracks visible.
- **Hold line for motion prompts:** `Hat, bandana and duster stay unchanged.`
- **Outdoors:** the hair and duster may move in the wind. Say so in the motion
  prompt, not in the character block.
- **No lips:** mouth-sync to the track is forgiving for him (see `grok-video.md`).

Removed from the old bible: the silver sheriff badge and the glowing red fissures.

## Story rules

1. Every video stands on its own. No storyline carries between videos.
2. Nothing recurs across videos except the cowboy. No running symbols, props or
   places (no badge, no ravens as a series motif). Inside one video, recurring
   objects are welcome: the story rules ask for one object that passes through
   at least three sections.
3. Endings are consequence or defiance. **Never redemption**, never warm or hopeful.
4. Tone follows the lyrics: earned coldness, quiet sovereignty, exile by choice.
   Fury is earned, not reactive.
5. Nordic and western elements may share one frame. That mix is the signature.
6. If a song's own notes say "not mythology-forward" (see `docs/song-projects.md`),
   keep the world plain and human: no Viking or western set dressing.

## World palette (open: any world is allowed)

The world is chosen fresh for every video and is never limited to this list. Any
place, era or setting is allowed: a hospital corridor, a 1970s motel, the ocean
floor, outer space, a modern city at dawn. The lists below are a starting point for
ideas, not a boundary. Concepts should use worlds outside this list as freely as
ones inside it. Only the cowboy and the story rules stay fixed.

Starting points:

- **Nordic:** Yggdrasil, longships, runes, wolves, ravens, frozen tundra, fjords.
- **Western:** burning plains, desert canyons, ruined saloons, wanted posters, the trail.
- **Apocalyptic:** collapsing neon cities in magenta, cyan and purple; falling towers.
- **Elemental:** green flame, orange firelight, blizzard, volcanic ash, embers.

## Look

- **Light:** name one hard source (fire, low sun, neon sign, doorway) and one dim fill.
  Firelight and deep shadow, but always a readable skull. Do not use mood words such as
  "dark", "moody" or "underexposed"; they darken the whole frame.
- **Color:** one or two subject colors against one or two background colors, such as
  rust orange and bone white against charcoal, or toxic green against black.
- **Texture:** one line, stated once: `35mm film still with fine grain`.
- **Camera:** locked camera and slow push-ins. Cut the extreme wide (small figure,
  fate) straight against the extreme close-up (skull, gloves, one object).
  Silhouettes against fire or low sun. A doorway or window as a frame in the frame.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9, set in the Grok composer, never written in the prompt.
- **Thumbnail frame:** a wide shot with a strong silhouette. The first shot of the
  video must contain the thumbnail's subject.

## Lyric tone (for reading songs)

- Narrators: the outlaw on the trail, the warrior who came back, the berserker,
  the chosen exile, the bounty hunter in frozen country.
- Lines are cold, ancient and self-contained. Emotion is shown, not explained.
- Joik passages are atmospheric counterpoint, not story. Treat them as texture
  (light, landscape, wind) rather than as lines to illustrate.
- Song list and themes: `docs/song-projects.md`.
