# Dark Nord — Song Projects Reference

Use this file to maintain continuity across all lyric sessions. Update when Tim approves final lyrics for any section.

---

## "Blood on the Trail"

**Theme:** Outlaw on the trail; blood carried in silence; fugitive or post-conflict figure.
**Vocalist:** Female lead vocal with joik atmospheric backing woven through.
**Rhyme scheme:** AABB
**Tone:** Cold, mythic, inevitable. Not grieving — beyond grieving.
**Established elements:** North Sámi joik vocals (Tim wrote authentic Sámi lyrics as atmospheric counterpoint to the outlaw narrative).

---

## "I AM A WARRIOR"

**Theme:** Warrior identity anthem. Cold, self-determined rage. Not rage at anyone — rage as a state of being.
**Tone:** Earned coldness. The coldest lines hit hardest. Short declarative lines.
**Key identity:** The narrator doesn't need to be believed. He simply is.

---

## "Outlander"

**Theme:** Chosen exile. Self-sovereignty. The narrator left by choice and would do it again.
**Tone:** Not wounded. Not bitter. Sovereign and still.
**Contrast:** Society is small. The outlander is large.

---

## "Owed Nothing"

**Theme:** Total emotional dismissal. Defiance and contempt. The narrator owes no one anything.
**Tone:** Ice-cold. No heat. The contempt is quiet, not loud.

---

## "Iron & Ash"

**Theme:** Berserker/Valhalla narrative. The warrior who burned and was reborn in battle.
**World elements:** Iron, ash, fire, Valhalla, ravens, the hall of the slain.
**Tone:** Epic and doom-forward. Heavier atmosphere than other songs.

## Bounty Hunter Song (untitled)

**Theme:** Fugitive pursuit narrative. Hunter tracks prey across frozen northern terrain.
**Signature:** Fire/ice juxtaposition — the hunter is fire, the terrain is ice, the prey is shadow.

## Alcohol Dependency Song (untitled)

**Theme:** Emotional contradiction of dependency. The thing that destroys is also the only comfort.
**Tone:** Raw and universal. NOT mythology-forward. No Viking or western overlay. Just the human condition.
**Key revision note:** Tim revised this away from mythology toward universality — keep it stripped of world-building.

## Notes on Joik Integration

Tim has demonstrated knowledge of authentic Sámi joik tradition. When writing joik-adjacent lines:
- Treat them as atmospheric/spiritual counterpoint to the main narrative
- They should feel ancient and non-narrative (joik describes, it doesn't tell a story)
- Use North Sámi phonetics if Tim has established specific words/phrases
- Mark clearly in the lyrics with `(joik vocal)` or `(joik backing)`
